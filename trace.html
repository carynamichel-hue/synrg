Trace the SynRG map

  1. Tap a spot below.
  2. Click around it on the picture, corner by corner. Click the first point again to close the outline.
  3. Restrooms are pins: tap Restroom, then click each spot (one at a time).
  4. Check-in, the entrance and the conference room are dots: drag a dot to move it (or tap its row and click the spot).
  5. To fix one: use the ✎ edit tool on the map's left (drag corners), the ✥ move tool, or 🗑 to delete.
  6. To move the names on the map, press Place the names (below) and drag them.
  7. Zoom the picture to how the schedule map should open and press Use this view.
  8. Press Copy for Claude and paste it in the chat.
Your work is saved in this browser as you go.

Place the names

Shows each name where it sits on the schedule map now, at the size a phone shows them. Drag a name to where you want it. Off its spot, it gets a line back to it — so a crowded name can sit out in open space. A name with a red ring touches another one.
